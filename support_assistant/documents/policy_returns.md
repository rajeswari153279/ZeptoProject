# Returns & Refunds Policy

Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in original packaging.
Perishable items and personal care products are not eligible for return.
Once a return is picked up, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued as store credit or bank transfer after verification.
Customers can initiate a return from the "My Orders" section by selecting the item and reason for return.
If an item is returned due to a seller error (wrong item, defective product), full refund including delivery charges is provided.
