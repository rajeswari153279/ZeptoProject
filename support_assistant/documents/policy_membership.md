# Membership Policy

Premium membership costs 199 per month or 999 per year, offering free delivery on all orders above 199.
Members get early access to sales events and exclusive member-only discounts.
Membership auto-renews unless cancelled at least 3 days before the renewal date.
A free 7-day trial is available for new users who have not previously subscribed.
Membership benefits are non-transferable and tied to a single registered account.
Cancelling membership does not affect orders already placed under active membership benefits.
