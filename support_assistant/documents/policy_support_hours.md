# Support Hours Policy

Customer support is available via chat and phone from 9 AM to 9 PM, all 7 days a week.
Email support is available 24/7 with a typical response time of 12-24 hours.
During major sale events, support hours are extended to 8 AM - 11 PM to handle higher query volume.
Premium members receive priority queue placement, reducing average wait time by up to 50%.
For urgent issues like payment failures, a dedicated helpline is available 24/7.
