# Gift Cards Policy

Gift cards are available in denominations of 500, 1000, and 2000, and never expire.
Gift cards can be purchased online and are delivered instantly via email as a redeemable code.
A gift card can be applied at checkout and combined with other payment methods for the remaining balance.
Gift card balance is non-refundable and cannot be converted to cash.
Lost gift card codes cannot be recovered unless the original purchase confirmation email is provided for verification.
