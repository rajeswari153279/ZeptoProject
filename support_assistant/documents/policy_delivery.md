# Delivery Policy

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within the city and 5-7 business days for other locations.
Express delivery is available for an additional fee and takes 1-2 business days.
Delivery is available all days except national holidays. Orders placed after 6 PM are processed the next business day.
Customers can track their delivery status in real time through the order tracking page.
If a delivery is delayed beyond the estimated window, customers are notified via SMS and email with an updated timeline.
Delivery partners will attempt delivery up to 3 times before returning the package to the warehouse.
