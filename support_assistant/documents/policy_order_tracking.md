# Order Tracking Policy

Every order generates a unique tracking ID sent via SMS and email upon confirmation.
Customers can view live tracking status on the website or app under "Track Order."
Tracking status updates include: Order Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered.
Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to reflect after dispatch due to courier system sync delays.
If tracking shows no movement for more than 48 hours, customers should contact support for investigation.
