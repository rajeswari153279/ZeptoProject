# Damaged / Missing Items Policy

If an item arrives damaged, customers must report it within 48 hours of delivery with photo evidence.
Missing items from an order should be reported within 24 hours through the "Report an Issue" option.
Verified damaged or missing item claims result in either a full refund or free replacement, customer's choice.
Claims reported after the specified window may not be eligible for replacement, at the discretion of the support team.
Customers are encouraged to record an unboxing video for high-value items as supporting evidence.
