# Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped.
Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled but can be returned after delivery following the returns policy.
To cancel, go to "My Orders," select the order, and click "Cancel Order."
Refunds for cancelled prepaid orders are processed within 3-5 business days.
Cash on Delivery orders that are cancelled require no refund action since no payment was made.
Repeated cancellations of Cash on Delivery orders may result in temporary restriction of the COD payment option.
